# Hello, world

This is the first post on the blog. The plan is to write about
science and technology mostly, with the occasional detour into
music or photography.

<!-- more -->

## Why a blog

Writing forces me to actually finish a thought. A note to self
sits half-formed for months; a post has to make sense to a stranger.
That's the whole pitch.

## What to expect

- Short posts when I learn something worth sharing
- Longer pieces when a problem deserves the depth
- No fixed schedule — quality over cadence
