# About Me

Hi, I'm Jonathan. I've always been deeply curious about how the world around me works.
In high school, most of my time was spent trying to understand the physical world.
I scratched my head over General Relativity and pretended I understood Quantum
Mechanics (I didn't). During college, my interest shifted towards understanding the digital
world.

I’m a software engineer based in Brooklyn, NYC. I currently work at
[Amazon Web Services](https://aws.amazon.com/) on the
[Python SDK](https://aws.amazon.com/sdk-for-python/) team, maintaining boto3 and
contributing to the next major version of the AWS SDK for Python. Most of my work sits
at the intersection of Python, developer tools, and the careful decisions that keep
software stable for people who depend on it.

I studied Computer Science at [Texas A&M University](https://www.tamu.edu/),
where I also spent time teaching, mentoring, and building software for student
organizations. Through [TAMU SHPE](https://www.tamushpe.org/), I led technical
projects connecting students, alumni, and recruiters. That work taught me how much
good software depends on understanding the people who use it, not just the code
behind it.

Music comes up a lot in what I build. I’ve made a
[Python SDK for Spotify](https://spotify-sdk.dev/) and
[Melody](https://www.my-melody.io/), an app that uses AI to surface insights from
your listening history. I also enjoy photography when I’m away from the screen.

This blog is where I'll write about things I’m learning and thinking about.

For a fuller picture of my work, visit [jonathangaytan.com](https://jonathangaytan.com/).
